# 📸 Teddy Eventz — Image Upload Guide

You can upload your photos as **either `.png` or `.jpg` / `.jpeg` / `.webp`**. The website now automatically detects and displays whichever format you upload!

---

## 1. 🧸 Brand Logo
Folder: `public/images/`
- `logo.png` (or `logo.jpeg`)

---

## 2. 🌟 Hero Banner Slideshow
Folder: `public/images/hero/`
- `hero-1.png` or `hero-1.jpg`
- `hero-2.png` or `hero-2.jpg`
- `hero-3.png` or `hero-3.jpg`

*(Recommended: Horizontal landscape photos, 1920x1080)*

---

## 3. 📖 About Us Section
Folder: `public/images/about/`
- `about-1.png` (or `about-1.jpg`) — Main portrait photo
- `about-2.png` (or `about-2.jpg`) — Overlapping photo
